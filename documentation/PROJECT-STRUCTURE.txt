JUTEORB WEBSITE FILE MAP

- index.html: homepage and only HTML page at the project root
- pages/: secondary storefront pages (About, Collections, Contact)
- assets/css/: shared storefront styles
- assets/js/: storefront interactions and product catalogue display
- assets/images/: hero slides, product images and founder imagery
- admin/: administrator dashboard, styles and scripts
- account/: sign-in and customer account prototype
- documentation/: image guide, project map and CRM dataflow diagrams
- presentation-assets/: screenshots and speaker notes
- juteorb/: original source/reference folders

The product catalogue currently stores product details and original-resolution images in this browser's local database. For public updates across customer devices, connect the catalogue to a shared server/API.
